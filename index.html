Quill-Gog

A private, single-user tool.

Quill-Gog is a personal utility operated by its owner for the owner's own use. It connects the gog command-line client to the owner's Google account so that a locally running assistant can read and write the owner's Google Docs and Google Drive files at the owner's direction.

It is not a public service. It has no other users, no sign-up, and no commercial purpose.
