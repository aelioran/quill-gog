Privacy Policy — Quill-Gog

Effective October 27, 2026

What this app is

Quill-Gog is a private, single-user tool operated by its owner for personal use. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the owner.

What it accesses

Through Google OAuth, the app accesses the owner's own Google Drive and Google Docs, solely so the owner's local tools can read and write the owner's files at the owner's direction. The app requests only the Drive and Docs scopes needed for that purpose.

What it collects and stores

The app collects nothing for its operator beyond what is needed to function. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer in an encrypted local keyring. File contents pass between Google and the owner's computer; they are not uploaded to, stored on, or processed by any third-party server operated by this app.

Sharing

No data accessed through this app is sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to any third party. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings (Third-party access), which immediately ends the app's ability to reach the owner's data.
